Itinerary:
Day 1 – Arrival in Baku
Arrival at Baku International Airport. Meet and greet by the local representative and transfer to the hotel. Check-in and relax. The rest of the day is at leisure.
Overnight stay in Baku.

Day 2 – Baku City Tour
After breakfast, proceed for a city tour covering the major attractions of Baku:
- Highland Park and Martyrs’ Lane
- Flame Towers viewpoint
- Baku Boulevard
- Little Venice
- Carpet Museum (outside view)
- Nizami Street
- Fountain Square
- Old City (Icherisheher)
- Maiden Tower (outside view)
- Shirvanshah Palace (outside view)
Return to the hotel. Overnight stay in Baku.

Day 3 – Absheron Peninsula Tour
After breakfast, proceed for an excursion to the Absheron Peninsula, covering:
- Ateshgah Fire Temple
- Yanardag (Burning Mountain)
- Baku’s famous fire-worshipping heritage and surrounding attractions
Return to the hotel. Overnight stay in Baku.

Day 4 – Full-Day Shahdag Tour
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Shahdag Mountain Resort.
- Enjoy the scenic mountain landscapes.
- Experience the cable car ride, subject to operation and weather conditions.
- Enjoy optional adventure activities available at the resort.
In the evening, return to Baku. Overnight stay in Baku.

Day 5 – Gobustan & Mud Volcanoes Tour
After breakfast, proceed for a full-day excursion to Gobustan National Park.
- Visit the Gobustan Rock Art Cultural Landscape and ancient petroglyphs.
- Explore the Gobustan Museum.
- Visit the Mud Volcanoes, subject to weather and road conditions.
Return to the hotel. Overnight stay in Baku.

Day 6 – Departure from Baku
After breakfast, check out from the hotel and transfer to Baku International Airport for your onward journey.
